隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日 · 适用于轻压 ZipLite iOS App

轻压（ZipLite）不会收集、上传或分享你的个人数据或文件。

我们处理什么

轻压只处理你主动通过系统文件选择器选择的压缩包、文件和文件夹。所有压缩与解压操作都在你的 iPhone/iPad 本地完成，不会发送到任何服务器。

我们不做什么

  • 不要求注册账号
  • 不申请网络权限
  • 不包含广告、分析、追踪或第三方统计 SDK
  • 不读取你未主动选择的文件
  • 不出售或共享任何数据

本地存储

导入的压缩包保存在应用沙盒的「轻压/Archives」，解压结果保存在「轻压/Extracted」。卸载应用会删除这些文件；如需长期保存，请导出到系统「文件」App。

儿童隐私

轻压不收集任何个人信息，适合所有年龄段用户。

联系我们

如对隐私政策有疑问，请通过 App Store 产品页的「App 支持」链接或通过 GitHub Issues 联系开发者。

English

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026 · Applies to ZipLite for iOS

ZipLite does not collect, upload or share your personal data or files.

What we process

ZipLite only processes the archives, files and folders you explicitly select with the system document picker. All compression and extraction happens locally on your iPhone or iPad and is never sent to a server.

What we never do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No network access
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs
  • No access to files you did not select
  • No selling or sharing of data

Local storage

Imported archives are stored in the app sandbox at ZipLite/Archives and extracted files at ZipLite/Extracted. Deleting the app removes these files. Export anything you want to keep to the Files app.

Children’s privacy

ZipLite collects no personal information and is suitable for all ages.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the App Support link on the App Store product page or open an issue in the GitHub repository.
