支持

光位 GoldenHour · iPhone / iPad

如何获得当前位置的光位？

首次打开时允许定位；也可以在设置中开启定位权限。未授权时会使用默认位置（上海）。

黄金时刻是什么？

太阳高度约在 -0.833° 到 6° 之间的时间段，光线柔和、色温偏暖，通常分为日出后和日落前两段。

时间为什么和我所在城市不同？

时间按设备当前时区显示。如果你查看的是其他城市的默认位置，请以当地时区理解时间。

会上传我的位置吗？

不会。所有计算在设备本地完成，详见隐私政策。

联系：请通过 App Store 产品页的「App 支持」或 GitHub Issues 联系开发者。

Support

GoldenHour · iPhone / iPad

How do I get the light for my location?

Allow location access on first launch or in Settings. If access is denied, GoldenHour uses Shanghai as a default.

What is golden hour?

The period when the sun is roughly between -0.833° and 6° above the horizon, with soft, warm light. There is usually a morning and an evening window.

Why are the times different from my city?

Times are shown in your device time zone. If you are viewing a default location in another city, interpret the times in that city's local time.

Do you upload my location?

No. All calculations run on device. See the Privacy Policy.

Contact: Use App Support on the App Store product page or open an issue in the GitHub repository.
