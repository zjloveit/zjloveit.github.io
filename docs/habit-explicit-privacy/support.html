Habit Explicit Support

Habit Explicit is a paid, ad-free checklist for daily habits.

FAQ

  • Does the app need the internet? No. Habits, check-offs, and reminders stay on your iPhone.
  • Does the app show ads? No. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Is there a subscription? No. Habit Explicit is a one-time App Store purchase.
  • Why does the app ask for notifications? Only to remind you to check today's list. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Is this a health or medical app? No. Habit Explicit is a personal productivity checklist. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
