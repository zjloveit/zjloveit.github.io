Sip Explicit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Sip Explicit ("the App") is a paid water tracker for iPhone. This policy explains how information is handled.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal data, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. It does not show ads and does not request tracking permission.

Hydration settings and sip history are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Notifications

If you enable daytime reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your iPhone. These alerts are not sent through our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App does not include advertising SDKs and does not require an account.

Contact

See Support or contact the developer via the App Store listing.
