Spend Explicit Support

Spend Explicit is a paid, ad-free spending log for a daily budget.

FAQ

  • Does the app need the internet? No. Budgets, purchases, and reminders stay on your iPhone.
  • Does the app show ads? No. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Is there a subscription? No. Spend Explicit is a one-time App Store purchase.
  • Why does the app ask for notifications? Only to remind you to log spending in the evening. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Is this financial advice? No. Spend Explicit is a personal spending log. It does not provide financial, tax, or investment advice.
