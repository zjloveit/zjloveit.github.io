隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日 · 适用于净界 PureBlock iOS App

净界不会收集、上传或分享你的个人数据、浏览记录或网页内容。

我们处理什么

净界是一个 Safari 内容拦截扩展。它在设备本地读取内置的拦截规则，并由 Safari 在网页加载时应用这些规则。App 不接收、不存储、也不上传你访问的网址或页面内容。

我们不做什么

  • 不要求注册账号
  • 不申请网络权限，不连接服务器
  • 不包含广告、分析、追踪或第三方统计 SDK
  • 不读取 Safari 浏览历史
  • 不出售或共享任何数据

本地数据

App 仅在你主动打开时显示本地规则数量与扩展启用状态。卸载 App 会删除应用内数据；Safari 中的扩展设置由系统管理。

儿童隐私

净界不收集任何个人信息，适合所有年龄段用户。

联系我们

如对隐私政策有疑问，请通过 App Store 产品页的「App 支持」链接或 GitHub Issues 联系开发者。

English

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026 · Applies to PureBlock for iOS

PureBlock does not collect, upload or share your personal data, browsing history or page content.

What we process

PureBlock is a Safari content blocker. It reads built-in blocking rules locally on your device and Safari applies them while pages load. The app never receives, stores or uploads the URLs you visit or page content.

What we never do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No network access and no server connection
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs
  • No access to Safari history
  • No selling or sharing of data

Local data

The app only shows the local rule count and extension status when you open it. Deleting the app removes its local data; Safari extension settings are managed by the system.

Children’s privacy

PureBlock collects no personal information and is suitable for all ages.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the App Support link on the App Store product page or open an issue in the GitHub repository.
