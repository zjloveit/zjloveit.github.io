Focus Explicit Support

Focus Explicit is a paid, ad-free timer for focus blocks and breaks.

FAQ

  • Does the timer need the internet? No. Timing and history stay on your iPhone.
  • Does the app show ads? No. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Is there a subscription? No. Focus Explicit is a one-time App Store purchase.
  • Why does the app ask for notifications? Only to tell you when a focus or break session ends. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • I changed the duration and nothing happened. Pause or finish the current session first. New lengths apply to the next block.
