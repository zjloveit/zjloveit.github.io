Wispcode QR Support

Wispcode QR helps you scan QR codes and create text, URL, and Wi-Fi codes.

FAQ

  • Does scanning need the internet? No. Scanning and generation work on your device.
  • Does the app show ads? No. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Why does the app ask for the camera? Only to scan QR codes. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Can I scan on the Simulator? No. Use a physical iPhone for the camera scanner.
