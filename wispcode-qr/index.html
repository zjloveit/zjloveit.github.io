Wispcode QR Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Wispcode QR ("the App") scans and creates QR codes on your iPhone. This policy explains how information is handled.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal data, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. It does not show ads and does not request tracking permission.

QR scanning and generation run on your device. Scan contents are not uploaded to our servers. Recent scans, if saved, stay in on-device storage.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan QR codes. The App does not record video or send camera frames off the device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not include advertising SDKs and does not require an account.

Contact

See Support or contact the developer via the App Store listing.
