Breathe Explicit Support

Breathe Explicit is a paid, ad-free guided breathing app for short daily sessions.

FAQ

  • Does the app need the internet? No. Sessions, goals, and reminders stay on your iPhone.
  • Does the app show ads? No. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Is there a subscription? No. Breathe Explicit is a one-time App Store purchase.
  • Why does the app ask for notifications? Only to remind you to take a breathing break. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Is this a medical device? No. Breathe Explicit is a personal breathing exercise tool. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
