Breathe Explicit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Breathe Explicit ("the App") is a paid guided breathing app for iPhone. This policy explains how information is handled.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal data, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. It does not show ads and does not request tracking permission.

Breathing settings and session history are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your iPhone. This alert is not sent through our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App does not include advertising SDKs and does not require an account.

Contact

See Support or contact the developer via the App Store listing.
