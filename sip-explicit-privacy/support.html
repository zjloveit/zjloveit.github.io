Sip Explicit Support

Sip Explicit is a paid, ad-free water tracker for daily hydration.

FAQ

  • Does the app need the internet? No. Goals, sips, and reminders stay on your iPhone.
  • Does the app show ads? No. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Is there a subscription? No. Sip Explicit is a one-time App Store purchase.
  • Why does the app ask for notifications? Only to remind you to drink during your waking hours. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Is this a medical device? No. Sip Explicit is a personal hydration log. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
