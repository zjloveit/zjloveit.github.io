支持

夜读 DarkLite · iPhone / iPad

如何在 Safari 中启用夜读？

设置 → Safari → 扩展 → 轻点「夜读」→ 打开开关，并选择「允许在所有网站」。

怎么按网站开关？

在 Safari 工具栏轻点 DarkLite 按钮，使用弹窗中的开关，或点「在此网站关闭」。

网站显示异常怎么办？

部分网站对颜色反转敏感。可在工具栏弹窗中关闭当前网站，或完全关闭扩展。

会上传浏览记录吗？

不会。夜读没有网络请求，开关状态只保存在本地。详见隐私政策。

联系：请通过 App Store 产品页的「App 支持」或 GitHub Issues 联系开发者。

Support

DarkLite · iPhone / iPad

How do I enable DarkLite in Safari?

Settings → Safari → Extensions → tap DarkLite → turn on the switch and choose “Allow on All Websites”.

How do I control a single site?

Tap the DarkLite button in Safari’s toolbar, use the popup switch, or choose “Turn off on this site”.

A site looks broken.

Some sites are sensitive to color inversion. Turn DarkLite off for that site from the toolbar popup, or disable the extension entirely.

Do you upload browsing history?

No. DarkLite has no network requests and stores settings locally. See the Privacy Policy.

Contact: Use App Support on the App Store product page or open an issue in the GitHub repository.
